# Global Connect Hub

Create a modern, responsive, professional business website for a company that sells book equipment, interpretation devices, conference headsets, audio systems, and electronic devices, while also providing professional interpretation and translation services.

The website should include:

 Home

 About Us

 Products

 Services

 Gallery

 Clients

 Contact

Product categories:

 Book Equipment

 Interpretation Equipment

 Conference Headsets

 Audio Systems

 Electronic Devices

Services:

 Simultaneous Interpretation

 Consecutive Interpretation

 Document Translation

 Conference Equipment Rental

 Technical Support

Design requirements:

 Clean and modern corporate style

 Blue, white, and gray color palette

 Fully responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop)

 Large hero banner with "Request a Quote" button

 Product cards with images and descriptions

 Contact form, WhatsApp button, Google Maps, and social media links

 SEO-friendly layout with fast loading

 Multi-language support (English, Portuguese, Tetum)
